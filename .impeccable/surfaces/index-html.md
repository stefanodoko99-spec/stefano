## Direction contract

THESIS: Stefano's portfolio is the day's penny broadside: every piece of work is a sheet that replaces the last, printed while you watch. It refuses the category default of big name, thumbnail grid and a "Let's talk" pill on dark minimal.

OWN-WORLD: coarse newsprint in one black relief ink; alarm red flooded only behind the engraving band and on the EXTRA contact sheet; ink-fade grey for what is not yet pressed. Wood-type words with rule underlines as controls, pressed state inks heavier; double rules, star-capped column rules, manicules, double-bordered boxes with small radii. A condensed wood gothic fitted line by line on its width axis, a nineteenth-century newspaper serif for the verse, a live WebGL relief engraving with form-following hatching, paper grain.

STORY: a business owner meets a designer whose own sheet is made to the letter, checks two working businesses (Elixir, an online perfume shop; Bar Martiri, a beach bar in Spille) in true colour through the loupe and the live links, and writes to him in their own language.

FIRST VIEWPORT: at 1440×900: toolbar strip under a double rule (STEFANO DOKO lockup left, WORK · ABOUT · CONTACT wood-type words, EN/SQ, black WRITE TO STEFANO ☞ button right); a dateline rule with the live Albania date and clock, sound and PROOF/PRINT words; STEFANO DOKO in fitted wood type across about 62% of the measure with GRAPHIC DESIGNER / BASED IN ALBANIA stacked right beside a manicule; the red engraving band (perfume shop left, beach bar right) about a third of the height; the three columns start above the fold. Primary action: the black toolbar button, repeated solid in the centre column. Signature interaction: the brayer, a 3D roller riding the scroll, inks each outlined headline as it passes; the pointer stirs the band's wet ink into two-plate misregistration. Motion grammar: hand-fed press judder on every state change, slit wipes as sheets feed, type-case tiles when the language is re-set.

FORM: Penny Broadside (posters-covers-sleeves-posada-calavera-broadside), dealt challenger 5 of 6 on the roll, taken by the user on the decision page over the assigned Press Check; seed key 13d2bca7; code-led.
